About the Instructor

Dr. Fatima Ouedraogo

Professor of Peace Studies, University of Ouagadougou
4.8 Instructor Rating 15,000+ Students 8 Courses

Dr. Ouedraogo has dedicated her career to peacebuilding across Africa. With over 15 years of experience working with communities in West Africa, she brings practical knowledge and academic expertise to her courses. She has mediated conflicts in Burkina Faso, Mali, and Niger, and has published extensively on African peace frameworks.

Her approach combines traditional African conflict resolution methods with modern peace studies theories, making her courses uniquely relevant to African contexts.
