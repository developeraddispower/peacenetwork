Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How do I enroll in a course?

Simply create a free account, browse our course catalog, and click "Enroll Now" on any course. All our courses are completely free!

How can I contribute my voice to "Our Book"?

Visit the "Our Book" page and fill out the contribution form. Your voice will be reviewed and added to our collective book of wisdom.

Is the platform really free?

Yes! The Pan-African Peace Network is completely free. We believe knowledge and unity should be accessible to all Africans.

How can I become a partner?

Contact us through the form above or email partners@panafricanpeace.org. We'd love to collaborate with organizations that share our vision.

Do you offer certificates?

Yes! Upon completing a course, you'll receive a free digital certificate that you can download and share.

How can I volunteer?

We welcome volunteers! Select "Volunteer Interest" in the subject dropdown above and tell us about your skills and interests.
